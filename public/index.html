Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services in Udaipur.

What makes VASTRIA dry cleaning different from others?

We use the latest European Hydrocarbon dry-cleaning technology, which is 100% eco-friendly and fabric-safe. Unlike harsh chemicals like PERC, our process ensures zero shrinkage, preserved shine, no foul chemical odor, and 99% stain removal guarantee.

How long does the cleaning process take?

Regular turnaround: Laundry & Wash – 48 hours, Dry Cleaning – 72 hours, Shoe Spa – 3 to 5 days. For urgent requirements, our Express Delivery option delivers your clothes within 24 hours.

Do you provide free pickup and delivery in Udaipur?

Yes, we provide free doorstep pickup and delivery across Udaipur (Savina, Hiran Magri, Fatehpura, Bhuwana, Sukher, City Area, etc.) for all orders above ₹300.

Is my delicate silk saree and designer suit safe?

Absolutely. We follow a strict 7-step inspection and treatment process. Silks, hand-embroidered lehengas, zari sarees, and woollens are individually tagged and processed in customized solvent cycles.
